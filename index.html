Leader of the Conservative Party of Woodstock Highschool

Gracelyn Grant

My name is Gracelyn, and I am your Conservative Party Leader for the 2026 WHS Mock Election. A little bit about me: I’m part student, part politician, and a full-time policymaker in progress for the people of New Brunswick. I am proud to be a New Brunswicker, and I believe in a province that values stability over radical change. I support a steady, predictable New Brunswick that focuses on preserving tradition, maintaining order, and building on what already works- no need to fix what isn’t broken. I value individual rights and believe people thrive when given opportunity, not when systems make things harder than they need to be. I’m especially interested in civic issues like the economy, healthcare, education, and the environment. As a teenager hoping to pursue a path in the medical and political fields, these issues are not just topics I study- they connect to my own future. I don’t just talk about challenges; I experience them alongside the people I hope to represent. I love public speaking, debating ideas, and taking on leadership roles. I’ve always been interested in how policies shape our everyday lives. Rest assured, I’m not afraid of a little friendly competition. I think we learn best when we’re willing to see things from another perspective. I’ll debate the opposition any day! To me, conservatism is more than a political ideology. It’s about staying true to your values, working hard, and believing that the future is shaped by the people willing to step up and take responsibility for it. Remember: Respect the Past, Build the RIGHT Future™ by voting Conservative.
